How it works

Four steps from idea to post. Nothing goes to your TikTok until you press Post.

  1. Connect TikTok

    Sign in with your TikTok account and approve access. We only read your basic profile (name and avatar).

  2. Generate a video

    Pick a theme like discipline, patience or new beginnings. MACHHAD creates a short vertical video with text, music and visuals.

  3. Review and edit

    Watch the preview, change the caption, and choose the privacy setting and whether comments, duets or stitches are allowed.

  4. Post

    Press Post to publish the video to your own TikTok profile. You can delete it from TikTok at any time.

Questions

Does MACHHAD post without asking me?

No. A video is only published after you review it and press Post. You can also save it to your TikTok inbox as a draft and finish it in the TikTok app.

What do you access on my TikTok account?

Your basic profile (display name and avatar) to show which account you are posting to, and permission to upload or post the videos you approve. We never read your messages, followers or other videos.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Videos are created for you and published on your own account.

How do I delete my data?

Disconnect MACHHAD in TikTok's settings, then email us and we delete your data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
