Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information MACHHAD ("we", "us") uses when you sign in with TikTok and post videos through our website at machhad-web.vercel.app, and how we protect it.

1. Information we receive from TikTok

When you choose "Continue with TikTok" and approve access, TikTok shares with us only what you allow:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your display name, username, avatar and a TikTok user ID. We show your name and avatar on the posting page so you know which account you are posting to.
  • Posting permissions (video.publish, video.upload): allow us to send a video you have reviewed to your own TikTok account, only after you press "Post to TikTok".
  • Posting settings from TikTok: the privacy options, comment/duet/stitch settings and maximum video length available for your account, so the posting page shows the correct choices.

We do not access your messages, followers, contacts, other videos, or your TikTok password.

2. How we use this information

  • To sign you in and show which account you are posting to.
  • To post the video you approved, with the caption, privacy level and settings you chose.
  • To show you whether the post succeeded.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described in section 4.

3. Storage and security

The access token TikTok gives us is kept in a secure, HTTP-only cookie in your browser. Page scripts cannot read it, and it expires automatically (usually within 24 hours). We do not store your TikTok profile or token in a database. All traffic uses HTTPS. Our app secret is kept on the server and is never sent to your browser.

4. Service providers

  • TikTok, to sign you in and publish your videos, under TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel, which hosts our website and may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.
  • AI tools used to create the motivational videos. No personal information from your TikTok account is sent to them.

5. AI-generated content

Videos made with MACHHAD are created with AI. They are labeled as AI-generated when posted to TikTok.

6. Your choices and data deletion

  • You decide if and when a video is posted, and who can see it.
  • You can remove MACHHAD's access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, App permissions.
  • You can delete posted videos directly in TikTok.
  • To ask what data we hold or to request deletion, email us. We reply within 3 working days.

7. Children

MACHHAD is not intended for people under 13, or under the minimum age to use TikTok in their country.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
